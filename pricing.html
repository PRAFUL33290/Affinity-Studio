Common Questions

Everything you need to know about our licensing.

Is this really a one-time payment?

Yes. Once you purchase a V2 license, it is yours to keep forever. There are no monthly fees or mandatory subscriptions required to access your tools or files.

Can I use the software on more than one computer?

As an individual, you can install Affinity apps on as many computers as you personally use for commercial purposes (as long as you are the only user).

What happens when V3 is released?

Your V2 license will continue to work indefinitely. While V3 will be a separate purchase, we typically offer significant upgrade discounts to existing users.

Do you offer educational discounts?

Absolutely. We offer special pricing for students and teachers. Please verify your status through our educational portal to see your specific pricing.
